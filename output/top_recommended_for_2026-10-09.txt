 HOW TO READ
   SETUP    the daily MACD histogram makes a HIGHER low while the price makes a LOWER (or equal)
            low: the two lows on different days, each on its own side of the other MACD dip, no
            day in between below the lower of the two lows, and the MACD on the recent low's day
            still above the earlier dip. The weekly candle of that price low touches the weekly
            EMA 11/22/50 band.
   DIPS     PREV / RECENT DIP = the dates of the two MACD-histogram troughs compared (daily chart)
            * after RECENT DIP = that dip is under 3 sessions old: its low and RSI use the
            sessions so far (it is judged now; the next run judges it again)
   ZONE WK  first trading day of the week whose candle is tested against the EMA band (weekly chart)
   RSI      the lowest RSI within 3 trading days of the recent dip, and its check:
              uncensored = it stayed at/above 30 (a healthier dip)
              censored   = it fell below 30 (oversold); still listed - RSI never removes a stock
   SCORE    strength versus the other ranked setups (0 = average, higher = stronger)
   2nd line (Sections 3 and 5) the spec's numbers, earlier -> recent: MACD at the two dips |
            LOW = the two price lows @ their dates | WEEK = the zone week's price range |
            EMA11/22/50 = the weekly averages that form the band
   Rs cr/d  median turnover of the last 20 trading days, in Rs crore per day
   (data to <date>) marks a stock whose data ends on another date than 2026-10-09
